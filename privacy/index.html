Blind Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Blind Timer is a party timer game published by Baran Mert Öztürk (MertheDev) for Android, iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the Blind Timer app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Blind Timer saves your language, timer control, sound, vibration and first-run guide preferences in local storage on your device. These preferences never leave your device. Player roles, target times and round results are kept only in the app's memory during a game and are not saved as a history.

Motion sensors

If you choose the orientation (hourglass) control, Blind Timer reads your device's motion sensor locally to start and stop the timer. Sensor readings are processed on the device only and are never stored or transmitted.

Audio and vibration

Blind Timer plays sounds that are bundled with the app and uses vibration when it is turned on. The app does not record audio and does not access the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

No accounts, advertising or analytics

Blind Timer has no user accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and the app does not send your preferences, sensor readings or game results to us or to anyone else.

App stores

When you download the app, Google Play or the Apple App Store may process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal data from them that identifies you.

Retention and deletion

Preferences stay on your device until you change them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Game data is removed when the app is closed.

Children

Blind Timer does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents or guardians can contact us with any questions.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.
