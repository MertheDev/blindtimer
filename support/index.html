Blind Timer Support

Blind Timer is a party timer game for Android, iPhone and iPad.

Questions, bug reports or feedback: merthedeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
Developer: Baran Mert Öztürk (MertheDev)

How do I play?

Agree on a target time together. Hold the phone with the screen facing away from you, start the timer and try to stop it exactly on target without looking. Then check how close you got, reset and pass the phone on.

How does the hourglass control work?

Open Settings (gear icon) and choose “Orientation · Hourglass”. Hold the phone in landscape, turn it to portrait to start and back to landscape to stop. Hold it steady; brief movement is ignored.

How does Imposter mode work?

Choose 3–10 players. Everyone privately reveals their card: one player is the Imposter and does not see the hidden target time. Follow the random playing order, take one blind try each, then compare the results and find the Imposter.

How do I reset the timer?

Long-press the screen or tap “Reset”. In Imposter mode, hold the screen to save your time and pass to the next player.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings (gear icon) and switch Sounds or Vibration off.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There is no account, and the game does not need an internet connection.
